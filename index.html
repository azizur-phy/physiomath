Syllabus: XI & XII Physics

Chapter index for the two papers. Chapters are covered in this order during the course.

Class XI: Physics 1st Paper

  1. Physical World and Measurement
  2. Vectors
  3. Dynamics
  4. Newtonian Mechanics
  5. Work, Energy and Power
  6. Gravitation and Gravity
  7. Structural Properties of Matter
  8. Periodic Motion
  9. Waves
  10. Ideal Gas and Kinetic Theory of Gases

Class XII: Physics 2nd Paper

  1. Thermodynamics
  2. Static Electricity
  3. Current Electricity
  4. Magnetic Effects of Current and Magnetism
  5. Electromagnetic Induction and Alternating Current
  6. Geometrical Optics
  7. Physical Optics
  8. Introduction to Modern Physics
  9. Atomic Models and Nuclear Physics
  10. Semiconductors and Electronics
  11. Astronomy
